What fires, and when

This is an illustrated timeline of the automation — a diagram built on this page, not a screenshot. It shows exactly what a real Lead Machine install does on every submission.

  1. 1 Immediately · 0:00

    Customer submits the quote form

    A customer asks for a quote on your form — from your website, your Google profile, or a link you text them.

  2. 2 Seconds later

    Customer gets an instant confirmation

    They know their request landed and that a real business is on it — before they go get a second quote.

    Illustrative example — customer email “Thanks — we've got your request. We'll review it and get back to you shortly.”
  3. 3 Same moment

    Owner gets a “New Lead” alert

    The owner's phone buzzes with who it is and what they want — no digging through a form inbox at the end of the day.

    Illustrative example — owner alert “New Lead: quote request just submitted — contact details and job notes inside.”
  4. 4 Same moment

    Lead lands on the dashboard

    Every lead becomes a row on a simple dashboard — name, contact, what they asked for, and its status — so nothing slips through the cracks.

  5. 5 Day 3, if it's still sitting

    A daily reminder until it's handled

    If a lead sits untouched for 3 days, the owner gets a daily reminder. Leads stop dying quietly in an inbox nobody opened.
